# 💡 LiteRight Academy — Simple Costing & Scaling Guide

> **Target Audience:** Anyone, even with zero technical or web development knowledge.  
> **Key Goal:** Clearly understand **what you pay**, **what you get for free**, and **how the cost scales as students join**.

---

## 🏛️ 1. The Simple Analogy (How Your Website Works)

Think of **LiteRight Academy** like setting up a high-end physical academy or showroom:

| Website Part | Physical World Equivalent | What It Does in Simple Words |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain Name** (`GoDaddy`) | **Your Shop Name & Signboard** | How students find you (`www.literiteacademy.com`). |
| **Frontend** (`Vercel`) | **The Showroom Interior & Display** | The beautiful black-and-white visual interface, buttons, and text. |
| **Backend API** (`Render`) | **The Academy Receptionist / Office** | Checks passwords, enrolls students, and grades quizzes. |
| **Database** (`Neon`) | **The Secure Filing Cabinet** | Stores student names, login details, and completed lessons. |
| **Video Engine** (`Cloudflare`) | **The Private Classroom Projector** | Plays high-definition video lessons smoothly without buffering or piracy. |
| **Email Service** (`Resend`) | **The Instant Postman** | Sends 6-digit OTP verification codes to students' inboxes. |

## 💰 2. Base Cost vs. Recurrence Cost

### A. Base Cost (Initial Baseline)
* **What you must pay upfront:** Only the **Domain Name** (`literiteacademy.com`).
* **Cost:** **~₹1,200 to ₹1,599 per YEAR** (which is only **~₹100 to ₹130 / month**).
* All software, code, and hosting services have **$0 setup fees**.

### B. Recurrence Cost (Monthly Running Cost)
You have two clear choices:

#### Choice 1: The 100% Free Bootstrap Plan (₹0 / month)
* **Monthly Cost:** **₹0 / month** ($0.00)
* **How it works:** You run on the generous free tiers of Vercel, Render, Neon, and Resend.
* **Catch:** When no one has visited the site for 15 minutes, the backend "sleeps". The very first visitor after that has to wait about 40–50 seconds for the server to wake up. *(Note: You can keep it awake for free using an automated 10-minute ping tool like UptimeRobot).*

#### Choice 2: The Recommended Pro Plan (~₹1,020 / month)
* **Monthly Cost:** **$12 / month** (**~₹1,020 / month**)
* **What you get:**
  1. **Render Web Service ($7/mo ≈ ₹595):** Server never sleeps; instant lightning-fast loading 24/7.
  2. **Cloudflare Stream ($5/mo ≈ ₹425):** 1,000 minutes of high-security video storage with adaptive streaming (1080p/720p/480p) that cannot be stolen with "Right-Click Save As".

| Service | Free Monthly Limit | What This Means in Real Life |

| **Vercel** *(Frontend)* | **100 GB** bandwidth / month | **100,000+ website visits** per month completely free. |
| **Neon** *(Database)* | **500 MB** storage | Holds account info and progress for **over 50,000 active students** completely free. |

| **Stage 2: Starter Pro** | **1,000 to 5,000** | **$12.00 / mo** | **~₹1,020 / mo** | Server runs 24/7 with zero lag + smooth Cloudflare video streaming. |

## 💵 5. Return on Investment (ROI) & Profit Math

Your course price is configured at **$199 (approx. ₹16,500)** per student:

* **If you sell to just 10 students in a year:**
  * **Gross Revenue:** ₹1,65,000
